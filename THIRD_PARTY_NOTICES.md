# Third-party sources and attribution

The analysis code and derived figures are distinct from the source databases and papers. This repository does not claim authorship of downloaded materials or replace their licences. Source files, attribution, cached response metadata and embedded copyright notices are retained.

## HAGR, GenAge and related resources

The principal sources are GenAge Build 21 (28 August 2023) and LongevityMap Build 3 (24 June 2017), with additional HAGR downloads described in their folder READMEs. HAGR credits João Pedro de Magalhães and its contributors. [HAGR terms](https://genomics.senescence.info/legal.html) identify CC BY 3.0 for covered content and separate GNU GPL terms for ARCT software. The original software notices remain applicable.

Reference: de Magalhães, J. P. et al. (2024). Human Ageing Genomic Resources: updates on key databases in ageing research. *Nucleic Acids Research* 52(D1), D900–D908. [doi:10.1093/nar/gkad927](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad927).

LongevityMap: Budovsky, A. et al. (2013). LongevityMap: a database of human genetic variants associated with longevity. *Trends in Genetics* 29, 559–560. Consult the HAGR terms for the additional database-specific citations when reusing the supplementary resources.

GenAge/LibAge reference caches and the pinned [HAGR-R snapshot](https://github.com/maglab/HAGR-R/tree/4e83b50142ea4b8c6625a7a86b6005ff3f606ed6/data-raw/GenAgeModel) preserve the original source identities. These are source snapshots, not newly authored database entries.

## OMA and other identifier resources

OMA All.May2026 provides the explicit orthology relationships. [OMA terms](https://omabrowser.org/oma/terms_of_use/) identify CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise noted; underlying sequences and annotations may have additional source terms. Cite the [OMA resource publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaa1007) when using these data. No OMA branding endorsement is implied.

Historical OrthoDB, Ensembl, NCBI and UniProt responses remain attributed through their original request URLs and metadata. Their source-specific notices apply; inclusion does not transfer ownership to the author of this analysis.

## Open Targets

The human association analysis uses Open Targets Platform data release 26.06, retrieved on 19 September 2026. [Open Targets data licensing](https://platform-docs.opentargets.org/licence) marks Platform data as CC0 1.0 and describes underlying-source licensing. [Terms of use](https://platform-docs.opentargets.org/licence/terms-of-use) and original evidence-source attribution remain applicable. Source API responses and per-datatype/per-datasource scores are retained.
