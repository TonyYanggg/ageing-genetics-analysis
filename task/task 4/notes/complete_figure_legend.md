# Task 4 supporting figure — complete four-organism overview

Presentation update, 26 September 2026: this overview supports the [complete all-gene atlas](all_gene_atlas_legend.md) and [six-family biological companion](expanded_figure_legend.md). Its analysis, counts and exports are unchanged.

**Figure | Direct ortholog pairs with GenAge lifespan evidence.** The complete four-organism analysis contains 206 distinct direct OMA All.May2026 species–Entrez-gene pairs with a qualifying GenAge Build 21 lifespan-effect annotation on both sides. They involve 320 distinct genes. These counts are pair and gene counts, respectively, not counts of independent orthogroups or conserved longevity mechanisms.

**a, Exact-ID mapping coverage.** Numerators are distinct GenAge genes accepted into the OMA protein-ID mapping; denominators are distinct genes with an Entrez identifier in each organism: budding yeast 895/911, nematode 858/886, fruit fly 191/200 and mouse 130/136. Mapping is a source-coverage measure, not the fraction with a longevity-associated ortholog. GenAge contains 2,138 records in these four organisms. The [all-nine coverage table](../results/complete_four_species/all_nine_coverage.csv) retains the other 67 records; Podospora and hamster lack OMA source representation.

**b, Direct pairs by organism.** Each upper-triangle cell counts qualifying pairs once: yeast–worm 97, yeast–fly 34, yeast–mouse 14, worm–fly 33, worm–mouse 16 and fly–mouse 12. Empty lower-triangle and diagonal cells are deliberately not comparisons. No missing pair is inferred through a third gene or interpreted as biological absence.

**c, GenAge direction annotations.** Bars partition each species-pair count into Anti/Anti (66 overall), Pro/Pro (34), discordant or mixed (32), and unresolved (74). “Unresolved” means the source has lifespan-effect evidence on both sides but lacks a clean two-sided Pro/Anti classification. These are catalogue annotations, not standardized experiments, effect sizes or independent biological replicates. No significance tests or error bars apply. The [six-row source table](../results/complete_four_species/species_pair_summary.csv) gives every plotted category value; the [complete 206-row table](../results/complete_four_species/all_qualifying_pairs.csv) gives genes, GenAge IDs, direct OMA relationships, lifespan-effect and direction annotations, and source provenance. [Gene multiplicity](../results/complete_four_species/gene_pair_multiplicity.csv) documents overlapping pairs: 67 genes have more than one partner, including 47 with partners in more than one organism.

**Interpretation.** The figure shows how much directly linked catalogue evidence is recoverable among the four principal models, and how heterogeneous its direction labels are. It does not estimate the prevalence of conserved longevity mechanisms. GenAge effects include different interventions and endpoints; one-to-many relationships and incomplete mapping constrain interpretation. The [three-example illustration](../figures/focused/focused_ortholog_examples.png) and its [paper-level legend](focused_figure_legend.md) provide targeted experiment context. In those examples, the mouse S6K1 effect is female-specific; the original mouse *Igf1r* lifespan increase was not reproduced in a later large cohort ([PMID 22132081](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22132081/)), though a later study reported genetic-background dependence ([PMID 23898955](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23898955/)); and the [2011 S6K1 correction](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=f39eea152043b5246fdfa4632451e33eaa76436f&repid=rep1&type=pdf) concerns a worm double-mutant strain rather than the mouse survival result. The mouse mTOR hypomorph study reports about 25% expression and about 20% greater median survival ([PMID 23994476](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3784301/)). The remaining catalogue-wide pairs have not each undergone primary-paper review.

**Methods and source.** The main table is a deterministic four-organism subset of the frozen [refined OMA pair audit](../results/oma_pairwise/refined/README.md). Each pair requires a direct source-supported OMA protein relationship, unambiguous accepted model-gene identifiers and at least one qualifying GenAge lifespan-effect record on both sides. Duplicate records and protein isoforms cannot add pair counts. The original all-nine audit includes 228 pairs across seven OMA-represented organisms; 22 lie outside this four-organism comparison. Python/matplotlib produced a 183 × 143 mm quantitative grid with editable SVG/PDF, PNG and 600-dpi TIFF. The [independent verifier](../scripts/verify_complete_four_species.py) reconciles the complete table, mapping coverage, gene multiplicity, all six figure comparisons and export bounds against frozen inputs.
