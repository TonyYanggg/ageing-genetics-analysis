# Scope revision and evidence boundaries

## Current scope

The 26 September 2026 revision presents every qualifying gene and direct ortholog pair across the four principal model organisms in a paginated atlas. The concise biological examples and all-nine-organism coverage audit remain supporting material. The current Task 4 README identifies canonical outputs and limitations.

## Earlier decisions

- 25 September 2026: expanded the analysis to all eligible direct ortholog pairs among the four principal organisms; retained the earlier three-example illustration.
- 24 September 2026: focused the biological presentation on selected, well-supported examples in the major model organisms. This focus does not establish biological absence in sparsely represented organisms.
- 18 September 2026: separated model-organism orthology, human disease associations and a proposed functional-enrichment workstream. Earlier human-anchor figures were reclassified as exploratory. Exact commissioning and approval claims were not established by original correspondence in the archive.

## Scientific boundaries

Task 4 concerns model-organism orthology and traceable lifespan evidence; human genes are excluded. Task 5 considers the complete human GenAge catalogue and disease associations. Functional enrichment was not pursued.

Historical human-anchor results remain reproducible under their original definitions but do not establish conserved genes, independent orthogroups or mechanisms. Shared symbols, shared mapped genes and pathway membership cannot substitute for explicit orthology. IGF1R and INSR must not be collapsed into an inferred orthogroup on that basis. A missing mapping is not evidence of biological absence.

No biological results were recalculated for the September scope correction. The current analysis uses explicit model-to-model orthology and documents source coverage separately. Supervisor approval of an earlier illustration must not be extended to later expansions or the complete atlas.
