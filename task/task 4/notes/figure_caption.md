# Figure caption

Status: **Exploratory human-anchor screen**, retained after the 2026-09-18 scope correction. This is not the main Task 4 orthology-based result. Its 66 anchors must not be described as 66 conserved genes or independent orthogroups. See [revised analysis plan](revised_analysis_plan.md).

**Cross-species longevity evidence in human-anchored homolog mappings.**
**a**, Fraction of unique GenAge Build 21 model genes annotated Pro-Longevity or Anti-Longevity that have a retained mapping in the frozen human-targeted ortholog export. Labels show mapped/eligible genes with nonmissing Entrez IDs: budding yeast 37/400, worm 379/858, fly 88/196 and mouse 79/130. Three directional records without Entrez IDs (two worm, one fly) are excluded from these denominators. **b**, Number of human gene anchors with directional evidence in zero to four model species, among 898 retained anchors. **c**, All nine anchors represented in three species, sorted alphabetically; no anchor has directional evidence in all four species. P and A denote Pro-Longevity and Anti-Longevity annotations; M indicates both within a species. A dash means no retained directional evidence, including unmapped or non-directional-only records. It does not establish absence of a homolog or longevity function. Pro/Anti refer to the gene's curated longevity influence, not the sign of every manipulation's lifespan effect.

The figure describes catalogue annotations, not experimental replicates. No statistical tests, error bars or inferential comparisons are applied. Human anchors can share model genes (for example, IGF1R and INSR both map to daf-2 and InR); counts are not independent orthogroups. Human GenAge membership is available in the table but is not counted as a fifth species of experimental longevity evidence. Four export rows involving two human identifiers with conflicting symbols are quarantined. The mapping algorithm, version and confidence are unavailable in this export, and species coverage is unequal. Functional conservation requires further orthology and experiment-level validation.

Source data: mapping_coverage.csv (a), species_count_distribution.csv (b), figure_displayed_candidates.csv and anchor_species_evidence.csv (c). The complete cross-species table contains 66 anchors, including the 57 represented in two species. Model evidence and human membership use GenAge Build 21 (28 August 2023); mapping uses the existing frozen website export, whose retrieval date is unverified.
