# Expanded Task 4 biological figure

Presentation update, 26 September 2026: this six-family figure is the concise experiment-level companion to the [complete all-gene atlas](all_gene_atlas_legend.md). Its selected evidence and exports are preserved.

**Fig. 4 | Selected longevity-associated orthologs across four major model organisms.** Six selected families connect 20 GenAge model genes through 21 direct OMA pairs. Columns show budding yeast (*Saccharomyces cerevisiae*), nematode (*Caenorhabditis elegans*), fruit fly (*Drosophila melanogaster*) and mouse (*Mus musculus*). Each cell gives one selected gene intervention, its reported lifespan outcome and a primary PMID. Green upward arrows indicate increased lifespan; brown downward arrows indicate decreased lifespan. Brown text also identifies loss or reduction of a longevity benefit in a particular genetic background. Colours encode the displayed experiment, not the catalogue's Pro/Anti category. DR means dietary restriction; CLS, chronological lifespan; KO, knockout; RNAi, RNA interference. No effect sizes are pooled and no new statistical test is performed.

**Orthology.** Direct relationships come from the frozen OMA All.May2026 analysis. TOR and SOD2 each have all six species pairs among the displayed genes. The yeast-related TOR pairs and worm-related SOD2 pairs have non-one-to-one cardinalities. ATG7 has all three one-to-one pairs among yeast, worm and fly. S6 kinase has worm–fly and fly–mouse pairs, with no direct worm–mouse pair recovered in this selection. AMPK has yeast–fly and worm–fly pairs, with no direct yeast–worm pair recovered. Insulin/IGF receptor has worm–fly and worm–mouse pairs; no direct fly–mouse pair was recovered. Mouse *Igf1r* is one of two OMA-linked receptor paralogs of worm *daf-2*; *Insr* is not displayed. Row membership never fills a missing edge by transitivity. These are selected biological families, not six source-defined OMA orthogroups or independent mechanisms. TOR and S6 kinase share a pathway and the same fly paper.

**Original examples.** TOR, S6 kinase and insulin/IGF receptor retain the gene, intervention, outcome and primary PMID from the approved three-row illustration. Female-specific mouse S6K1 and original Igf1r increases have asterisks; the corresponding male increases were not significant. The original Igf1r female result was not reproduced in a later large cohort ([PMID 22132081](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22132081/)); genetic-background dependence was proposed subsequently ([PMID 23898955](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23898955/)). This is a cautionary example, not an established general three-species mechanism. The [original legend](focused_figure_legend.md) retains all ten primary gene–experiment references and the S6K1 correction: a worm double-mutant strain lacked the intended rsks-1 allele; rebuilt-strain experiments confirmed the lifespan epistasis result but revised growth/reproduction interpretation. The correction does not change mouse survival results.

**AMPK.** High-copy yeast *SNF1* expression shortened mean generational (replicative) lifespan ([10921902](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10921902/), targeted results checked through the indexed primary full text). Extra worm *aak-2* copies increased mean lifespan ([15574588](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15574588/), Figure 2 results checked). Adult fly AMPK expression in fat body or muscle extended lifespan ([23312286](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23312286/), abstract). The fly symbol SNF1A is retained from frozen GenAge. These experiments differ in endpoint, tissue and dosage; the selected yeast result does not summarize all SNF1 experiments or chronological ageing. Chronological yeast evidence can differ ([28373292](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28373292/)).

**ATG7.** Yeast ATG7 deletion reduced chronological survival in tested synthetic media ([19302372](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19302372/), abstract and figure captions). Worm Ce-atg7 knockdown impaired the longevity phenotype of dietary-restricted *eat-2(ad1113)* mutants ([17912023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17912023/), abstract); the figure deliberately labels that background. Fly Atg7-null adults were short-lived ([18056421](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18056421/), abstract). Worm GenAge's Increase/Unclear annotation is preserved in the source table rather than rewritten to match this selected experiment. Other primary work reports atg-7 RNAi lifespan shortening ([17204841](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17204841/)) and a broader autophagy RNAi study reports timing/background-dependent effects ([19469880](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19469880/)). The latter abstract does not resolve each atg-7 condition, so it is context, not a gene-specific counterexperiment claimed here.

**SOD2.** Yeast SOD2 deletion abolished *sch9Δ* chronological lifespan extension ([12586694](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12586694/), abstract). This is a background-specific requirement; the paper's joint SOD1/SOD2 overexpression result is not attributed to SOD2 alone. Worm sod-2 deletion extended lifespan but reduced lifespan in an *isp-1* background ([19197346](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19197346/), abstract). Fly Sod2 RNAi produced early adult mortality ([12456885](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12456885/), abstract). Mouse conditional Sod2 deficiency in connective-tissue mesenchymal cells reduced survival with premature ageing-related phenotypes ([21108731](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21108731/), abstract). This is not a global heterozygous knockout or a normal-ageing lifespan-extension experiment. The row illustrates context dependence rather than a uniform antioxidant benefit.

**Selection and limitations.** The [selection rationale](expanded_selection.md) records the screen and editorial choices. All 206 qualifying pairs remain in the supporting catalogue; six selected families cannot estimate the prevalence of conserved longevity effects. Empty cells mean no experiment selected, not absence of an ortholog or lifespan evidence. Differences in yeast replicative versus chronological assays, tissue specificity, developmental injury, sex, genotype and experiment quality constrain causal comparisons. Primary review is targeted and mostly abstract-level, with the stated full-text exceptions; it is not a systematic assessment of all studies or later replications.

[Selected direct pairs](../results/expanded/selected_oma_pairs.csv) retain all frozen relationship fields. Run `scripts/build_expanded_examples.py` and `scripts/verify_expanded_examples.py`. Four exports are 183 × 247 mm; TIFF is 600 dpi and SVG text remains editable.

**Suggested review sentence.** Selected orthologous genes have experimental lifespan evidence across major model organisms, spanning nutrient signalling, autophagy and oxidative stress, but intervention and organism context can change the direction and interpretation of the effect.
