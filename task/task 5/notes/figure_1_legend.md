# Task 5 review figure legend

**Figure 1 | Direct disease and phenotype associations across the complete GenAge human-gene catalogue.** **a,** All 306 genes with a current Ensembl target, ranked by the number of unique direct target–term relations in Open Targets Platform release 26.06. Blue points indicate 126 genes with one or more separately matched LongevityMap study records; grey points indicate 180 genes without a matched record. LongevityMap status is an annotation and was not used for inclusion. **b,** Analysis scope and mutually exclusive classification of 422,250 unique direct relations. All 307 GenAge Build 21 genes were retained; MT-CO1 lacked an Ensembl target for this query route and is reported as unqueryable, not as having no disease association. Terms were classified using ontology namespace and Open Targets top-level categories. **c,** Reverse cumulative distribution of the Open Targets overall association score. The median (0.0326) and 90th percentile (0.0660) are descriptive; 1,383 zero-score relations remain in the denominator but cannot be placed on the logarithmic axis. Open Targets scores are neither probabilities nor causal effect sizes. **d,** Numbers of unique gene–term pairs carrying each Open Targets evidence datatype; labels also report the number of GenAge genes represented. A pair can contribute to more than one datatype. No inferential test was performed because the panels describe the complete resource-bounded catalogue rather than a sampled comparison.

Source data: `results/figure_1_source_data/`. Full relations and evidence scores: `results/opentargets_gene_disease.csv`, `results/opentargets_datatype_scores.csv` and `results/opentargets_datasource_scores.csv`. The analysis uses only direct Open Targets associations (`enableIndirect: false`) and does not establish disease causality, therapeutic direction, an ageing mechanism or an effect on human lifespan.
